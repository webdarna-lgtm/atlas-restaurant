Our Story

Crafting Moments
Worth Remembering

Atlas was born from a singular vision: to create a dining destination that transcends the ordinary. Chef Karim Bensouda, trained across the finest kitchens of Paris, Tokyo, and New York, returned to Morocco to weave his heritage into every plate.

Each evening at Atlas is a carefully orchestrated symphony of flavor, texture, and memory — where Moroccan soul meets contemporary mastery.
